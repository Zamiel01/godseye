What is a Data Breach?

A data breach occurs when unauthorized individuals gain access to confidential information stored by organizations. This can include your passwords, email addresses, personal details, financial information, and other sensitive data.

When companies get hacked, millions of user accounts can be compromised simultaneously. Cybercriminals then sell this information on the dark web, where it's used for identity theft, financial fraud, and other malicious activities.

The most dangerous part? You might not even know your data has been stolen until it's too late.
